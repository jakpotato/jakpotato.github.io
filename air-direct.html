Questions We Get Asked

Frequently questioned.

How does the air arrive if there is no packaging?

We believe packaging is unnecessary when the product is already naturally present in the environment. Your delivery animal will arrive with the air and release it in the general vicinity of your residence.

What animal will deliver my air?

Our proprietary GeoFauna™ system selects an animal based on your approximate location. Possible couriers include raccoons, geese, pigeons, squirrels, foxes, llamas, goats and, in unusually fortunate circumstances, a horse.

Do I have to take care of the delivery animal?

Absolutely not. However, we are also not responsible for removing the animal after delivery. Please do not attempt to return it to us. It probably knows where it came from.

Can I return my air?

Yes! Air can be returned for a refund. Customers must either pay an additional delivery fee or supply an appropriate animal capable of transporting the air back to us.

What happens if my animal eats the air?

We classify this as a Courier Consumption Event™. Unfortunately, consumed air is not eligible for replacement.
